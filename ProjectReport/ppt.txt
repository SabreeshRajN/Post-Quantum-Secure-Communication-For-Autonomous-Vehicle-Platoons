Department of CSE (AIML) 
Ensuring the authenticity of communicating vehicles and the
integrity of safety-critical messages remains a major challenge
in autonomous vehicle platoon networks. As quantum
computing threatens the security of classical digital signature
algorithms, a quantum-resistant authentication mechanism is
essential 
to 
establish 
trust 
and 
prevent 
unauthorized
communication.

Proposed System / Methodology
1. Vehicle Registration:
Generates Dilithium public/private key pairs.
Shares public keys through the trusted server.
2. Message Authentication:
Signs outgoing platoon messages using the Dilithium private
key.
Attaches the digital signature to each message.
3. Signature Verification:
Verifies received signatures using the sender's public key.
Accepts valid messages and rejects forged or modified
messages.

Conclusion
The proposed authentication framework addresses the need for
quantum-resistant vehicle authentication in autonomous platoon
networks using CRYSTALS-Dilithium.
The implementation enables secure vehicle identity verification,
message authenticity, and integrity through post-quantum digital
signatures.
Experimental validation demonstrates successful signature
generation and verification, effectively preventing message forgery
and impersonation attacks.
This work extends the secure communication framework previously
developed and establishes a foundation for trusted, quantum-secure
autonomous vehicle communication.
